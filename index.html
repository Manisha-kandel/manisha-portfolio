Research & Publications

2024 Conference Paper

Graph-Structured Analysis of Grade Crossing Inventory Systems for Freight Rail Safety

Kandel, M. et al.

Applies Graph Neural Network architectures to the Federal Grade Crossing Inventory System (GCIS) dataset, modeling grade crossings as nodes within the broader freight rail network graph. The research identifies structural vulnerability patterns and predictive risk indicators across thousands of at-grade intersections, enabling data-driven prioritization of safety interventions in national rail infrastructure.

GNN Graph Analysis Rail Safety GCIS
2023 Working Paper

Spatiotemporal Patterns in Freight Rail Network Disruptions: A GNN Approach

Kandel, M. et al.

Investigates spatiotemporal propagation of disruptions across the U.S. freight rail network using PyTorch Geometric. Constructs a dynamic graph representation of track segments and junction nodes, applying temporal GNN layers to learn how localized failures cascade through interconnected rail corridors over time.
